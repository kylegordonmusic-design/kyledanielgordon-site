Work

Kyle

Orchestration, pop string arrangements and conducting for composers, artists and labels, from first sketch to final session.

Recent work includes the orchestrations for Rosalía's LUX with the London Symphony Orchestra, orchestration on Oppenheimer and Bridgerton, and arrangements for the BBC Proms. Projects can start at any stage: a mockup that needs a full score, an album that needs an orchestra, or a session that needs a conductor.

How a project runs

Any stage
01

Sketch to score

A mockup, sketch or demo becomes a full score written for the players in the room, in the composer’s voice.

02

Score to stand

Scores and parts are proofed and delivered before the session, so studio time goes to the music.

03

Stand to session

On the podium or in the booth, keeping the session on schedule and the takes clean.
