About

Los Angeles

Kyle Daniel Gordon is an orchestrator, arranger and conductor based in Los Angeles. He was supervising orchestrator on Rosalía's LUX, recorded with the London Symphony Orchestra, which won six Latin Grammys including Album of the Year and received ten Grammy nominations.

In film and television he orchestrated Oppenheimer and Bridgerton, and has worked in the music departments of The Mandalorian, Black Panther: Wakanda Forever and Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker. He leads orchestration teams in Los Angeles for composers including John Williams, Ludwig Göransson, Kris Bowers, Walter Murphy and Justin Hurwitz.

At the BBC Proms he was lead orchestrator on Soul Revolution (2025) with the BBC Concert Orchestra, writing its finale and playing the Royal Albert Hall organ. In 2026 he arranged the Bond and Beyond Prom, performing his arrangement of Radiohead’s “Spectre” on the organ, and wrote arrangements for Marvin Gaye: Prince of Soul. His piece The Little Bird was premiered by the San Francisco Symphony in 2026. He arranged, orchestrated and conducted the Baby Shark 10th Anniversary symphonic album with the LSO. Romani: The Untold Story, which he orchestrated and conducted with the LSO and Dallas String Quartet, received the 2024 Billboard Award for #1 Classical Album. He has conducted sessions and concerts in Los Angeles, Nashville and London, and arranged touring orchestral shows for Hawthorne Heights and Escape the Fate.

Trained in jazz and classical music, he holds a master's in music from the University of North Texas, and made his Carnegie Hall debut as a bassist in 2018. He is also credited as Kyle Gordon.
