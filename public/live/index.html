BBC Proms · San Francisco SymphonyLive

Arrangements, new music and the occasional turn at the Royal Albert Hall organ.

At the BBC Proms, Kyle Daniel Gordon was lead orchestrator on Soul Revolution (2025) with the BBC Concert Orchestra, writing its finale and playing the Royal Albert Hall organ. In 2026 he arranged for Bond and Beyond, performing his arrangement of Radiohead’s “Spectre” on the organ, and contributed arrangements to Marvin Gaye: Prince of Soul. His piece The Little Bird was premiered by the San Francisco Symphony in May 2026.
