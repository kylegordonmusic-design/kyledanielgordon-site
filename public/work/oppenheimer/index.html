Work / Film / Oppenheimer

Christopher Nolan · 2023Oppenheimer

Orchestration on Ludwig Göransson’s Academy Award-winning score.

Kyle Daniel Gordon was an orchestrator on Ludwig Göransson’s score for Oppenheimer, Christopher Nolan’s 2023 film about J. Robert Oppenheimer. The score won the Academy Award for Best Original Score.

Nolan asked for a score led by the solo violin, which he heard as Oppenheimer’s own voice. Around two and a half hours of music runs through the three-hour film, and the orchestra recorded it in five days.
