David Friesen · Origin Records · 2024This Light Has No Darkness

A 12-part work by bassist David Friesen, arranged and orchestrated by Kyle Daniel Gordon for 33-piece chamber orchestra.

This Light Has No Darkness, Volume 1 sets bassist David Friesen’s compositions for a 33-piece chamber orchestra, arranged and orchestrated by Kyle Daniel Gordon. Origin Records released it on 16 February 2024.

The suite was first conceived for the National Academic Symphonic Band of Ukraine. On the album the orchestra plays with Friesen, pianist Paul Lees and percussionists Charlie Doggett and Rob Moore.
