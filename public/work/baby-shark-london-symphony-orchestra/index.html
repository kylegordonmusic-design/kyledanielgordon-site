Credits / Records / Baby Shark

Pinkfong · London Symphony Orchestra · 2025Baby Shark × LSO

The London Symphony Orchestra version of “Baby Shark”, arranged and conducted by Kyle Daniel Gordon.

For the tenth anniversary of “Baby Shark”, The Pinkfong Company recorded a new version with the London Symphony Orchestra at LSO St Luke’s in London. Kyle Daniel Gordon arranged and conducted it. Pinkfong released it on 19 September 2025, as a single and on the Baby Shark 10 Years vinyl album.

It was the London Symphony Orchestra’s first collaboration with a Korean character brand. Gordon also conducted the Bebefinn Medley with London Symphony Orchestra, released on 14 November 2025.
