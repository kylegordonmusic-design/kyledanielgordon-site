Work / Records / Rosalía · Español

RosalíaLUX

Orchestrations for an album sung in thirteen languages, with the London Symphony Orchestra on every track.

LUX is Rosalía's fourth studio album, released in November 2025. Kyle Daniel Gordon was the album’s lead orchestrator. The album was recorded with the London Symphony Orchestra at AIR Studios in London, conducted by Daníel Bjarnason. Gordon also arranged about an hour of choral music for the project, performed by the Escolania de Montserrat.

LUX won six Latin Grammys at the 27th Latin Grammy Awards, including Album of the Year, and is nominated for Album of the Year at the 69th Grammy Awards.

Rolling Stone ‘Art for Art’s Sake’: Rosalía’s Collaborators Reflect on ‘Lux’ Gordon tells the story of the call from the London Symphony Orchestra that brought him onto the album.

Questions

About LUX
Who orchestrated Rosalía’s LUX?

Kyle Daniel Gordon, credited as Kyle Gordon. The album’s official credits on rosalia.com read “Orchestrated by Kyle Gordon.” He was lead orchestrator on LUX, writing the orchestra parts the London Symphony Orchestra recorded at AIR Studios in London. Other arrangers, including Caroline Shaw and Angélica Negrón, are credited on some tracks.

Who conducted the orchestra on LUX?

Daníel Bjarnason conducted the London Symphony Orchestra sessions at AIR Studios, London.

What has LUX won?

LUX won Album of the Year at the 2026 Latin Grammys and has 10 nominations at the 69th Grammy Awards, including Album of the Year.

Is Kyle Daniel Gordon the comedian Kyle Gordon?

No. Kyle Daniel Gordon, the orchestrator, arranger and conductor based in Los Angeles, is a different person from the comedian and musician Kyle Gordon.
