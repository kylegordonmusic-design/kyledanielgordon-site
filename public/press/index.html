Programme · 150 words

Kyle Daniel Gordon is an orchestrator, arranger and conductor based in Los Angeles. He was supervising orchestrator on Rosalía’s LUX (2025), recorded with the London Symphony Orchestra, which won six Latin Grammys including Album of the Year. In film and television he orchestrated Ludwig Göransson’s score for Oppenheimer and has worked on Frozen II, Star Wars: The Last Jedi, Black Panther: Wakanda Forever, The Mandalorian and The Gilded Age. At the BBC Proms he was lead orchestrator on Soul Revolution (2025) and arranged for Bond and Beyond (2026), playing his arrangement of Radiohead’s “Spectre” on the Royal Albert Hall organ. His piece The Little Bird was premiered by the San Francisco Symphony in 2026. He holds a master’s in music from the University of North Texas.
