Kyle Daniel Gordon is a Los Angeles orchestrator, arranger and conductor. He has worked in the orchestration and music teams on film and television scores including Oppenheimer, Frozen II, Star Wars: The Last Jedi, Black Panther: Wakanda Forever, The Mandalorian and Bridgerton, and was lead orchestrator on Rosalía’s LUX. On Oppenheimer he was one of the orchestrators on Ludwig Göransson’s score. Credits from before 2026 list him as Kyle Gordon (IMDb, album notes, streaming services); it’s the same person.
