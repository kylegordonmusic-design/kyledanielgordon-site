Rental fees

Per chart, per run

Good to know

Can I see the score before booking?

Yes. Request a perusal score for any chart and we’ll send a watermarked PDF, usually within one business day.

What if my vocalist needs a different key?

Kyle can re-key any chart for your soloist for a fee. We’ll quote it before anything is booked.

Do we need extra licensing?

The rental covers the arrangement. Performance rights for the underlying songs go through your orchestra’s usual ASCAP/BMI licenses. Recording, broadcast and streaming are arranged separately.

How are parts delivered?

As print-ready PDFs (score and parts), or printed and shipped to you, with shipping billed at cost.

Our orchestra is smaller (or different) than the listed instrumentation.

Kyle can adjust the instrumentation to fit your orchestra, for a fee: fewer winds or brass, no rhythm section, strings only, added choir, and so on. Tell us your forces and we’ll send a quote. Some charts already work with reduced forces as written, and we’ll tell you which.
